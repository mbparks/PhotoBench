No watermark. JPEG uses a white background for transparency. Camera metadata, including location, is not copied. A project JSON preserves editable settings.

A small photographic workbench.

Open → Adjust → Compare → Export. Start with a look, then tune the sliders. All editing happens on your device.

Magic Eraser: Retouch → Magic Eraser works in both modes. Paint the whole object and a small margin, use Subtract mask to tidy the selection, then choose Erase object. Inspect the preview and Keep result, Try another fill, or go Back to mask. Cancel stops processing and keeps your mask. Kept removals are reversible and stored with project JSON. Surrounding-pixel fills work best on simple backgrounds and repeated textures; they cannot know what was truly hidden.

Creative filters: Finish → Creative filters has Halation, Grunge, Film, Dehaze, and Chromatic aberration in both modes. Select a filter and raise its strength; Apply bypasses it while keeping settings. Fine-tune opens extra controls. Negative Dehaze adds haze; negative Chromatic aberration reverses the fringe. Film palettes are artistic interpretations, not measured film-stock emulations.

Easy mode covers everyday editing. Advanced adds curves, the color mixer, perspective and expansion, selective points, editable brushes, and double exposure. Retouch is available in both modes.

  • Undo / redo: Ctrl or ⌘ Z / Shift Z. Up to 50 edits in this session.
  • Compare: click Before / after or hold the spacebar while the canvas has focus. Original tones share the current geometry. Choose a named snapshot in Edits to compare a complete earlier composition.
  • Crop: drag a corner to resize, drag inside a selection to move, or drag outside to draw a rectangle. Ratio and percentage fields offer precise control. Apply to keep it.
  • Selective: add a point and adjust its radius and tone. Drag a point to reposition it. Numeric positions work with a keyboard.
  • Brush: select an adjustment, then paint. Select a recorded stroke to change its radius, strength, or feather; bypass it, erase part of its mask, or delete it. Erase All removes all adjustment strokes. Pen pressure can control the radius of new strokes.
  • Clone: pick a clean source, then paint over a blemish. Each stroke samples an offset from that source. This is a manual clone tool, not content-aware healing.
  • Zoom: choose Full detail to inspect working-resolution pixels. Use Pan and drag, pinch with two fingers, or Ctrl/⌘ + wheel to zoom. Double-click toggles Fit/100% outside painting tools. On a large desktop, drag the controls divider or use its arrow keys.
  • Recipes: Project → Export / apply recipe transfers a global look between photos. Geometry, text, and frame are optional. Recipes contain no images.
  • Edits: bypass editing groups, revisit session undo states, and capture up to eight named snapshots. Snapshots are saved with the project and retain any second images they use.
  • Geometry: horizontal/vertical perspective correction can leave exposed edges. Choose transparent, solid, or stretched-edge fill. Expand canvas also offers mirrored fill. Local masks remain attached to the source photo.

Working limits: JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 40 MB. Images over 20 megapixels or 6000 pixels on an edge are reduced on import, with notice. Expanded output is limited to 32 megapixels and 8192 pixels per edge. RAW/HEIC, automatic subject masks, generative scene reconstruction, and batch export are not included in v1.4.

Fast preview starts from a source reduced to 1280 pixels. Full detail renders the working resolution within output limits and can take longer. Exports render independently of preview quality. Soft masks, grain, and detail can vary slightly with resolution. Nine-anchor curves support linear and smooth interpolation. Work is 8-bit browser RGB, not a color-managed RAW workflow.

The standalone HTML works without a connection. On a server, place sw.js beside index.html to enable offline reload over HTTPS or localhost. Autosave is device and browser specific. Projects from v1.0–v1.3 import into v1.4; new schema-4 projects require v1.4 to retain removal patches. Recipes remain schema 2 and do not transfer spatial removals. Close old editor tabs when updating your installation.
